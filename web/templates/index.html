About this app

The Monty Hall problem is a probability puzzle: a car is behind one of three doors and goats behind the others. You pick a door, the host (who knows what is behind each door) opens a goat door, then you may stay on your original pick or switch to the other closed door.

How to use it

  • Click Door 1–3 to start. The host reveals a goat behind another door.
  • Choose Stay or Switch to see whether you win the car.
  • Run simulation plays {{ simulation_count | int }} random rounds and shows approximate win rates (switching wins about twice as often as staying).
